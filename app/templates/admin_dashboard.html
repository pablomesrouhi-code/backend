Profit calculator — COD (USD)

Funnel: leads × confirmation → confirmed × delivery → delivered. Revenue = delivered × AOV. Lifetime AOV (SAR) from orders DB; USD = SAR ÷ FX rate.

Loading store baseline…

Revenue & product (from store AOV)

Avg pieces/order = mean sum(offer_qty) per order. Selling price/piece = AOV ÷ avg pieces.

Funnel & ad spend

Confirmation = % of leads that confirm. Delivery = % of confirmed delivered (rest = returns).

1 — Breakeven

Per 1 lead: max CPL before you lose money (ops + COGS + COD revenue).

2 — Profit at scale

Same assumptions × number of leads (ad spend = leads × CPL).

COGS = confirmed × avg pieces × product cost. Fulfillment fee per confirmed warehouse shipment. Configure SAR_PER_USD in backend env for FX.
