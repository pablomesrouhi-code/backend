Funnel & ad spend

Confirmation = % of leads that confirm. Delivery = % of confirmed delivered (rest = returns).

1 — Breakeven

Per 1 lead: max CPL before you lose money (ops + COGS + COD revenue).

2 — Profit at scale

Same assumptions × number of leads (ad spend = leads × CPL).

COGS = confirmed × avg main pieces × product cost. COD fees: confirmation + delivery + return + warehouse (USD, env COD_FEE_CONFIRMATION_USD, COD_FEE_DELIVERY_USD, COD_FEE_RETURN_USD, COD_FEE_WAREHOUSE_USD). Configure SAR_PER_USD for FX.
